Our Story

A Family Tradition of
Excellence

For nearly four decades, Beyti has been bringing the authentic flavors of Turkish to our community. Every dish tells a story of tradition, passion, and the finest ingredients sourced from local farms and Turkish importers.

Founded by the Turkey family in 1945, our restaurant continues to honor the recipes passed down through generations while embracing modern culinary techniques.
